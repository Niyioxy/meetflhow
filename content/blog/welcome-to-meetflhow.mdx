Most teams spend hours every week in meetings, and even more time afterward trying to remember what was actually decided. Someone volunteers to take notes, the notes go stale in a doc nobody reopens, and action items quietly disappear.

We built MeetFlhow to close that gap automatically.

## What MeetFlhow does

Record a meeting — from your browser, a Chrome extension on a live call, an uploaded file, or the mobile app — and MeetFlhow takes it from there:

- **Transcribes it**, with automatic speaker identification.
- **Summarizes it**, pulling out decisions, open questions, and action items with owners.
- **Scores it**, with a Meeting Coach that tracks engagement, clarity, and balance over time.
- **Shares it**, straight into Slack, Notion, Jira, or Linear.

No one has to remember to write anything down.

## Why now

We're opening MeetFlhow up for anyone to try, free, with 5 recordings a month per workspace and full access to every feature. If your team is tired of meetings that evaporate the moment everyone hangs up, [give it a try](/login).

We'll be using this blog to share product updates, playbooks on running better meetings, and lessons learned building MeetFlhow. More soon.
